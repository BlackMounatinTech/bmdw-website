Our Story

Built from nothing.

Four years ago, Michael walked into Home Depot, bought a shovel and a rake, and started Black Mountain Dirt Works. One truck. One phone. A lot of hours and a lot of learning along the way. The business has grown fast since then on repeat customers, referrals, and steady work across Vancouver Island.

The same guy who started this company is the one answering your call today.

Hands-on and owner-operated from day one. When you call Black Mountain, you're talking to the person who'll be on site. It keeps things direct and makes it easier to figure things out as the project moves along.
