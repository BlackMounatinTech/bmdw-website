Backyard Restoration

This backyard was in rough shape when we showed up, waist-high weeds, lumpy ground, the kind of yard you wouldn't let a dog out in. The homeowners wanted to reset the whole thing. We scraped it down to bare dirt, pulled out everything that didn't belong, leveled out the surface, and brought in clean topsoil across the whole area. Last step was hydroseed, blown-on seed mix that establishes faster than throwing down regular grass seed. A few weeks later they had a clean, even, green lawn. Sometimes the best move is to just wipe the slate clean and start over.
