Residential Land Clearing

This homeowner hadn't been able to use more than half of their own backyard in years, the growth had just taken over. Trees, brush, blackberry, everything. We came in with the excavator and went to work clearing it out piece by piece. Stumps came up, brush got hauled, and by the time we were done they had most of their yard back. Watching someone walk around parts of their own property they hadn't been able to set foot on in a decade is one of the more satisfying parts of the job.
