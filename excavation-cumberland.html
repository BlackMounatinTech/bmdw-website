Excavation & Landscaping in Cumberland

Serving Cumberland and surrounding areas on Vancouver Island.

Cumberland has seen a surge of growth in recent years. What was once a quiet village is now attracting families and builders looking for a community feel with easy access to the Comox Valley. Many properties here sit on sloped, forested lots that need clearing and careful site prep. Retaining walls, drainage solutions, and driveway construction are some of the most common projects we take on in Cumberland.

Black Mountain Dirt Works is an owner-operated excavation and landscaping company serving Cumberland and all of Vancouver Island. We work directly with homeowners and property owners on everything from backyard transformations to full site preparation for new builds. We try to approach every job the same way, carefully, and with the same attention we'd put into our own place.
