Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us.

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Black Mountain Dirt Works ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. It applies to information collected through our website, our online forms, and our advertising on platforms like Facebook and Instagram.

Information we collect

When you contact us or submit a quote request or lead form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your location or project address
  • Details about the project you're inquiring about

How we use your information

We use the information you give us only to:

  • Contact you about your project and provide a quote
  • Respond to your questions and schedule work
  • Follow up with you about services you've asked about

We may contact you by phone, text message, or email using the contact details you provide.

How we protect and share your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not share it with anyone except as needed to deliver our services to you (for example, scheduling or communication tools we use to run our business) or where required by law.

Information collected through advertising

If you submit a form through one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram, that information is provided to us through Meta's lead tools and is handled according to this policy. Meta's own use of your data is governed by Meta's privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time, and you can request that we delete the information we hold about you. Just reach out using the contact details below.
